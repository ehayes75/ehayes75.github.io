Outreach and Mentoring

  • Co-Organizer of Undergraduate GEMS (2025-present)
    - U-GEMs is an undergrad organization dedicated to uplifting math students who identify as gender minorities. We host a variety of monthly events including professional development workshops, social events, panels, etc.
  • Graduate Student Mentor for MxM project (Spring 2026)
    - I was the Graduate student mentor for the Madison Experimental Labs project Developing model of HIV infection that correlates with treatment induced immune recovery". I facilitated group meetings, taught some differential equations concepts, and generally helped guide the undergraduate students through the process of doing research, giving presentations, and writing a final report.
    - My students' project is going to be featured in the UW Math department newsletter! Check back for a link to the article.
  • Member of the Committee for TA Policies and Procedures (CTAPP) (2025-present)
    - I serve on a committee comprised of graduate students and professors whose purpose is to review TA performance by analyzing student evaluations. CTAPP also advises on any changes to the TA position.
